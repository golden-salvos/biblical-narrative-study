> *Questions raised during the session, with answers drawn from the teaching.*

---

### Q: Does the "third" language mean the judgment doesn't cover the whole earth?

Yes. Each of the first four trumpets strikes a third: a third of the earth, the sea, the rivers, the light. A third affected leaves two-thirds untouched, and that proportion is the text's way of marking a target. The destruction is wide-scale, and a third of the earth is an enormous region, but it is not universal.

The third that is struck corresponds to the center of the Antichrist's kingdom in the Middle East. The untouched remainder is the audience God intends to witness what happens to those who have rebelled. The distinction between target and audience is the point of the proportion.

---

### Q (Audience contribution): Is the "star" of the fifth trumpet an angel?

Yes. A literal star is a sun, and if one struck the earth there would be no earth left. The star in Revelation 9:1 is given a key and opens the shaft of the abyss, which are the actions of a person rather than a celestial body.

The grammar adds to it. John does not see a star falling; he sees a star that *had* fallen, a completed fall. An angel who has already fallen is a fallen angel, and by the time of the trumpets the fallen angels have already been expelled from heaven. *(The expulsion itself will be explored when the study reaches Revelation 12.)*

---

### Q (Audience contribution): Does it matter which specific being receives the key, or whether releasing it works against what God is doing?

It doesn't, and that's the point. The Lord is sovereign and directing the entire sequence; the fallen angel who receives the key is not acting on his own initiative. Ezekiel 38:4 describes the same dynamic with Gog: the Lord says he will put hooks in Gog's jaws and pull him out to attack Jerusalem — the adversary supplies the aggression, but the timing and purpose belong to God. Releasing a demonic power does not defeat God's purpose; it puts a box around what that power is permitted to do, so that even its rebellion serves the outcome God has already determined.

---

### Q: Are the locusts attacking Israel?

They harm anyone in their operating region who lacks the seal of God, and that region is the Antichrist's domain, which includes Israel. Israel sits at its center. Israelites who are not sealed are therefore exposed to the torment along with everyone else in the region. The seal protects the 144,000 specifically. It does not shield the nation as a whole.

---

### Q: Are the 144,000 saved? If so, why do they need to be sealed?

They are not saved at this point, and that is why the sealing is needed. If the 144,000 were Christians at the return of the Lord, they would have been gathered to heaven with the church and would not be on the earth to be sealed at all. They are Jews who do not yet believe in Jesus when the wrath begins. The seal keeps them alive through it so that they can serve as witnesses inside the Millennial Kingdom.

They do come to faith before the Millennial Kingdom begins. *(The 144,000 will be revisited when the study reaches Revelation 14.)*

---

### Q: Doesn't it seem unjust for God to seal a specific number of people and protect them while others suffer?

From a human standpoint the selection can look arbitrary. The objection assumes God chooses with the limited knowledge a human being would have. He does not. He knows from before the beginning who will be written in the Book of Life, and the sealing reflects what he already knows about the 144,000 and where their story ends. The question is being asked from a human view of the situation; God is answering it from complete knowledge of the outcome.

The same objection can be raised about Egypt: Egyptians who suffered under the plagues while Israel was spared. It has the same answer. And it points to something the study returns to repeatedly: justice, in the full sense, will not exist on this earth until the Lord comes.

An audience member illustrated the point from the courts. Deals struck for testimony routinely give leniency to one guilty party in exchange for evidence against another, even when everyone in the room knows who did more. Human systems, working as designed, still cannot deliver justice.

---

### Q: Were people worshiping creation, and is that why it is taken away?

No. The people in view were not worshiping the trees, the sea, or the rivers. They were failing to worship the God who created them, and they were worshiping demons instead. The first four trumpets take away the gifts of creation because those gifts were never received with thanks. The fifth and sixth trumpets then release the beings they actually served. The sequence moves from what was ignored to what was chosen.

---

### Q: Why isn't this material taught more widely in churches?

Teaching that presses on judgment, suffering, and accountability tends to thin an audience rather than fill it. Material that does not draw a crowd is often left alone for that reason.

But the material cannot be studied honestly without changing how a person sees God. It is impossible to see him revealed in his Word and not see him differently. And once the picture is clear, there is rest in it: he has it covered, and what he asks is that each person be about his kingdom and bring as many people into it as possible.

---

### Q: Isn't what is happening now the groundwork for the Antichrist to arrive as a savior?

Yes. He arrives as a conquering hero, not as an obvious villain. Peace has not been brought to the Middle East in two thousand years, and he will bring it. He will also allow the Jews to rebuild the temple. Hostility toward Israel is already rising, including in places that have historically treated Israel as an ally.

The events Jesus called birth pains in Matthew 24 have been occurring periodically for two thousand years. What makes them birth pains is that they intensify and come closer together, so their presence now is expected.

He will not announce himself. He operates in the shadows, and there will be no moment when someone declares this is the date or this is the man. What will be unmistakable is the effect of the covenant with many: peace, and with peace, Israel resuming sacrifices on the Temple Mount. That is the sure sign that the covenant has been confirmed. At the midpoint of the seven years, three and a half years later, he is revealed.

---

### Q: Will the Antichrist be human?

Yes, ultimately human, though with demonic influence. Beyond that, whether he has special capabilities is speculation the text does not invite.
