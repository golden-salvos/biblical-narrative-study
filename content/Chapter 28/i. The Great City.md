## Overview

Revelation 17 and 18 finally explain the Babylon whose fall was announced back in chapter 14. An angel shows John a woman dressed as a queen, riding a scarlet beast with seven heads and ten horns, drunk with the blood of the saints, and then interprets the vision himself: the heads are seven mountains and seven kings, the horns are ten kings who will hand their power to the beast, and the woman is "the great city that has dominion over the kings of the earth." Chapter 18 then mourns that city's sudden destruction through the eyes of the kings, merchants, and sailors who grew rich from her. This session works through the angel's interpretation, explains how this study identifies the seven heads and the beast that "was, and is not, and is to come," and then, holding current events loosely, weighs which city in today's world might fit the portrait.

## 1. Recap

The previous session covered Revelation 14 through 16, a series of visions with no timestamps, each placed on the timeline by its own clues. Right after the [[Glossary#Abomination of Desolation|Abomination of Desolation]], three angels warn the whole earth: worship the Creator, Babylon is fallen, and anyone who takes the [[Glossary#Mark of the Beast|mark of the beast]] will drink the wine of God's wrath. The grain harvest gathers the church, and the grape harvest treads the Antichrist's army in the winepress. Then the [[Glossary#Seven Bowls of Wrath|seven bowls]] are poured out, ending at the seventh bowl, when the great city splits into three parts and God remembers [[Glossary#Babylon the Great|Babylon the Great]].

The second angel announced Babylon's fall before Revelation had said anything about who or what Babylon is. Revelation 17 and 18 explain it. The previous session closed on three questions about Babylon and the beast, which are two separate entities. How do they interact? How do they stay in agreement? And why do they eventually turn on each other?

## 2. Two Figures: The Woman and the Beast

*"Then one of the seven angels who had the seven bowls came and said to me, 'Come, I will show you the judgment of the great prostitute who is seated on many waters, with whom the kings of the earth have committed sexual immorality, and with the wine of whose sexual immorality the dwellers on earth have become drunk.' And he carried me away in the Spirit into a wilderness, and I saw a woman sitting on a scarlet beast that was full of blasphemous names, and it had seven heads and ten horns. The woman was arrayed in purple and scarlet, and adorned with gold and jewels and pearls, holding in her hand a golden cup full of abominations and the impurities of her sexual immorality. And on her forehead was written a name of mystery: 'Babylon the great, mother of prostitutes and of earth's abominations.' And I saw the woman, drunk with the blood of the saints, the blood of the martyrs of Jesus. When I saw her, I marveled greatly."* (Revelation 17:1–6)

The vision has two characters. The first is a woman, the [[Glossary#Great Prostitute|great prostitute]], dressed as royalty and carrying a golden cup. The second is the beast she rides, which has seven heads and ten horns. That should sound familiar: it is the anatomy of the dragon in Revelation 12 and the [[Glossary#Beast from the Sea|beast from the sea]] in Revelation 13.

The arrangement of the scene says something before the angel explains anything. The woman is riding and the beast is carrying her. There is a partnership between them, and she appears to be the one in charge. She looks like the queen, the one holding the reins.

## 3. The Angel Explains the Mystery

John marvels, and the angel does not leave him to guess.

*"But the angel said to me, 'Why do you marvel? I will tell you the mystery of the woman, and of the beast with seven heads and ten horns that carries her. The beast that you saw was, and is not, and is about to rise from the bottomless pit and go to destruction. And the dwellers on earth whose names have not been written in the book of life from the foundation of the world will marvel to see the beast, because it was and is not and is to come. This calls for a mind with wisdom: the seven heads are seven mountains on which the woman is seated; they are also seven kings, five of whom have fallen, one is, the other has not yet come, and when he does come he must remain only a little while. As for the beast that was and is not, it is an eighth but it belongs to the seven, and it goes to destruction. And the ten horns that you saw are ten kings who have not yet received royal power, but they are to receive authority as kings for one hour, together with the beast. These are of one mind, and they hand over their power and authority to the beast. They will make war on the Lamb, and the Lamb will conquer them, for he is Lord of lords and King of kings, and those with him are called and chosen and faithful.'"* (Revelation 17:7–14)

*"And the angel said to me, 'The waters that you saw, where the prostitute is seated, are peoples and multitudes and nations and languages. And the ten horns that you saw, they and the beast will hate the prostitute. They will make her desolate and naked, and devour her flesh and burn her up with fire, for God has put it into their hearts to carry out his purpose by being of one mind and handing over their royal power to the beast, until the words of God are fulfilled. And the woman that you saw is the great city that has dominion over the kings of the earth.'"* (Revelation 17:15–18)

This chapter interprets its own symbols, and most of the work is done by the angel. The single most important line is the last one. The woman is the great city that has dominion over the kings of the earth. Any attempt to identify Babylon has to start there. In this study's reading, the text does not describe a religious system or an abstract spirit of the age. It describes a city, a great one, and it says that city has influence over the rulers of the world.

That line, together with the "seven mountains on which the woman is seated" (17:9), is why most interpreters through history, ancient and modern, have identified Babylon with Rome, the famous city of seven hills that ruled the kings of the earth in John's day; Peter already uses "Babylon" as a code name for Rome (1 Peter 5:13). Others see Babylon as a symbol of every godless world system, or expect a literal rebuilt Babylon in Iraq. This study takes a different view, developed below: the seven mountains are kingdoms, and the city is a future one at the center of the Antichrist's kingdom.

> **Key Principle:** Revelation 17 explains its own imagery. Before reaching for a theory about Babylon, read what the angel says she is: a great city with dominion over the kings of the earth.

## 4. The Seven Heads: The Kingdoms That Came for the Seed

The study has already met these [[Glossary#Seven Heads and Ten Horns|seven heads]]. In Revelation 12 they sat on the dragon, Satan himself. The angel now identifies them twice: they are seven mountains, and they are seven kings. In the prophets, a mountain can stand for a kingdom (Daniel 2:35, 44–45; Jeremiah 51:25), and in this study's reading that is how the mountains should be read here.

What do these kingdoms have in common? They are the empires recorded in Scripture that tried to destroy Israel. They were after more than Jerusalem. They were after the nation through which the promised seed would come. From Genesis 3:15 forward, Satan has been trying to destroy the seed, and every one of these empires was his instrument for doing it.

The list begins before Israel was even a nation. **Egypt** tried to destroy the Hebrews in slavery. **Assyria** carried the northern kingdom into exile. **Babylon** carried the southern kingdom into exile. **Medo-Persia**, present-day Iran, conquered Babylon; it inherited the exiles, and although Cyrus allowed them to return, it was under Persian rule that Haman plotted to annihilate the Jews (Esther 3). **Greece** followed, and under [[Glossary#Antiochus IV Epiphanes|Antiochus IV Epiphanes]] it tried to stamp out Jewish worship altogether. **Rome** destroyed the temple and Jerusalem in AD 70.

Now read the angel's count from John's vantage point. *Five have fallen*: Egypt, Assyria, Babylon, Medo-Persia, and Greece were all gone by the time John wrote. *One is*: Rome ruled the world while John sat on Patmos. *The other has not yet come*: the seventh had not yet arisen in John's day. That seventh kingdom is the [[Glossary#Islamic Caliphate|Islamic Caliphate]]. After the western Roman Empire dissolved and the eastern empire weakened, the Arab world was united under a single ruler, and that rule spread across the Middle East and North Africa and into Europe.

## 5. The Eighth: The Beast That Was, and Is Not, and Is to Come

Then the angel adds an eighth. The beast that was and is not is itself an eighth king, but it belongs to the seven.

That phrase, *was, and is not, and is to come*, describes a kingdom that existed, ceased to exist, and will exist again. The study has identified it as the caliphate. The caliphate was formally abolished in 1924, only about a hundred years ago. It was. It is not. It is going to return. This is the same reality Revelation 13 described as a head with a mortal wound that was healed: a kingdom that died and comes back to life.

Why does the eighth belong to the seven? The answer is who has been driving all of them. The [[Glossary#Antichrist|Antichrist]] cannot be behind all seven kingdoms, because he is a man, born at one time and place and operating in the future. Satan is not limited that way. He has been operating since the beginning, and every one of these empires has been animated by the same spirit. The Antichrist rises at a particular point in history, but his kingdom belongs to the seven because of who is driving him. The seventh is Islam, and the eighth is also Islam: the historical caliphate, and the same caliphate revived.

Satan is driving both sides of the picture: the ten kings who come together into one, and the beast they hand their power to. *(There is some nuance in how Satan operates through each, which this session did not develop further.)*

> **Key Principle:** The seven heads span all of history because they belong to Satan, who is not bound by time. The ten horns belong to one moment, because they belong to the Antichrist, who is.

## 6. The Ten Horns: Ten Kings With Royal Power

The ten horns are not kingdoms. They are [[Glossary#Ten Kings|ten kings]]. The seven heads are empires spread across history. The ten kings exist together, in one time and place, and that time is still future.

Daniel 7 has already given the sequence. Ten kings arise in the last kingdom. The little horn, the Antichrist, rises up among them and subdues three of them (Daniel 7:8, 24). The study has already identified those three: Egypt, the king of the South, and the nations on either side of it, Libya to the west and Sudan, biblical Cush, to the south (Daniel 11:42–43). The rest then hand him their power, and he becomes the sole ruler over all of it. Revelation 17:13 confirms it: they are of one mind, and they hand over their power and authority to the beast.

Pay attention to the wording. They *have not yet received royal power*, but they *are to receive authority as kings*. In this study's reading, royal power is not taken by force, and it is not won in an election. It is inherited through a family line. These are not presidents or prime ministers. Nor is it military power, which is power taken by force. They are kings, and a king holds absolute authority in a way Americans, who have never lived under a king, are not used to imagining.

That detail matters when the clues are laid over the Middle East. Saudi Arabia is not ruled by a president; it is ruled by a king of the house of Saud. Jordan is ruled by a king. Egypt is ruled by a president who came to power through a military coup in 2013. Turkey is ruled by an elected president. Any of this could change quickly. What the text requires is a moment when ten rulers of the region hold royal power together, even if only *for one hour*, before handing it to the beast. They then make war on the Lamb, and the Lamb conquers them.

## 7. The Woman: A City Over Every Nation

Then the angel turns back to the woman. The waters she sits on are *peoples and multitudes and nations and languages*. That is everyone. She sits over the entire earth, which means her influence reaches the entire earth. She is the mother of prostitutes, presented as a queen in royal robes.

Is there a queen with royal authority over the whole world? No. So her dominion has to be something other than a throne. The text answers it in the same breath: the kings of the earth have committed immorality with her, and the merchants of the earth have grown rich from her. Her dominion is influence, the kind that wealth and commerce buy.

Now the relationship between the two figures comes clear. The woman rides the beast and seems to be driving it. But the ten horns and the beast will hate her. They will strip her, devour her flesh, and burn her with fire. The partnership is real, and it ends in betrayal.

And God is behind the betrayal. *God has put it into their hearts to carry out his purpose* (Revelation 17:17). The kings think they are acting on their own hatred. They are fulfilling the words of God.

## 8. How Close Are These Things?

In this study's view, these times are close. No date can be set, but the pieces are in place in a way they have never been before.

One marker settles a great deal. Right now Jews have no legal authority to sacrifice on the Temple Mount. In this study's reading, when the temple is being built and the sacrifices start again in Jerusalem, the seven years have already begun. The [[Glossary#Covenant with Many|covenant with many]] will already have been confirmed, because that covenant is what opens the way for the sacrifices. The restart of sacrifice does not mark the start of the seven years; it shows that the seven years are already underway.

The rules governing the Temple Mount are already loosening. Jewish prayer, long barred there under the "status quo" arrangement, has in recent years reportedly been tolerated by Israeli police on parts of the site. At the time of this session in 2026, the study's own estimate was that sacrifice could be permitted within a year or so. This is not something decades away.

> **Key Principle:** When sacrifice resumes in Jerusalem, the seven years are not about to begin. They have already begun.

## 9. Searching for the Great City

If the beast that was and is not and will come again is the caliphate, then the great city has to sit at the center of its world. Babylon is also the center of the Antichrist's kingdom, and that is the clue to follow.

### The Center of Islam

What nation is the center of Islam? Saudi Arabia. Islam was born there. Mecca and Medina are there, and Muhammad was born in Mecca. Muhammad is a historical figure, mentioned not only in Muslim tradition but in Christian and Jewish sources written within a few years of his death.

Mecca is the holiest site in Islam. Every Muslim on the planet is required, if able, to make the pilgrimage there once in a lifetime, the [[Glossary#Hajj|Hajj]], and to circle the Kaaba, the cube-shaped shrine at the center of its great mosque. Medina, the city where Muhammad ruled and is buried, is the second holiest. Both are bound to Muhammad.

The Qur'an is not like the Bible. It is not a narrative history but a collection of recitations, arranged largely by length rather than chronology, and many readers coming from the Bible find it hard to follow. Islam's picture of the end times, drawn mostly from the hadith (the traditions about Muhammad), runs remarkably parallel to Revelation, as a mirror image. In this study's reading, the one Islam expects, the [[Glossary#Mahdi|Mahdi]], who will unite all the Islamic peoples, is the one the Bible calls the Antichrist.

The third holiest site in Islam is in Jerusalem: the Temple Mount. The [[Glossary#Dome of the Rock|Dome of the Rock]] stands there. It is a shrine, a monument, rather than a mosque, and it is built over the rock that Jewish tradition identifies as Mount Moriah, where Abraham bound Isaac. The al-Aqsa Mosque stands at the southern end of the same platform. Islam venerates the site because tradition identifies it as "the farthest mosque" to which Muhammad was carried in his Night Journey (Qur'an 17:1). The Qur'an also tells the story of Abraham's sacrifice without naming the son, and most Muslim scholars identify him as Ishmael, the firstborn (see [[Glossary#Akedah|Akedah]]).

That brings the study back to Ishmael. The oldest son of Abraham became the father of the Arab peoples. One distinction keeps this clear. Arab is a people, a bloodline. Islam is a religion. Most of the Arab world follows Islam, but the two are not the same thing. Iran, for example, is Persian, not Arab.

### Who Has Held Jerusalem

Within a few years of Muhammad's death, Islamic armies began conquering the Middle East, all of North Africa, and on into Spain, pressing into Europe until they were stopped at Tours in 732. Jerusalem came under Islamic rule in the 630s, and it remained under Muslim rulers for most of the next thirteen centuries.

That is where the [[Glossary#Crusades|Crusades]] came from. They were fought in the Middle Ages, in several waves, by the Christian kingdoms of Europe, launched in answer to a Byzantine appeal for help against the Seljuk Turks and aimed above all at taking back Jerusalem and the Holy Land from Islamic rule. The First Crusade captured the city in 1099, but it was lost again to Saladin in 1187. Historians still debate the Crusades' motives, and their conduct included grievous atrocities, including massacres of Muslims and Jews; but they were a response to centuries of Islamic conquest, not an unprovoked attack. Jerusalem was not finally taken out of Islamic hands until British forces captured it in December 1917, during the First World War.

The Ottoman Empire held the caliphate until its defeat by the Allies in the First World War. Out of that collapse, Atatürk came to power in Turkey, declared it a secular state, exiled the last caliph, and abolished the caliphate in 1924.

Britain then governed the land and, with the rise of Zionism, laid the groundwork that led to Israel's rebirth as a nation in 1948. But Israel did not hold the Old City of Jerusalem until the Six-Day War in 1967, and even now it does not have full control. The Temple Mount remains under the administration of an Islamic trust overseen by the king of Jordan, a role Israel recognized by treaty.

### A Small Country With an Outsized Reach

The woman has influence over every nation, and the kings of the earth are entangled with her. Which country fits that description? Answering it is not a matter of Scripture. It is a matter of how the world actually works right now, and much of that is not reported openly.

Start with the dollar. In 1971 President Nixon ended the dollar's convertibility to gold, and the dollar became a free-floating currency no longer backed by gold. The problem was how to keep the world wanting it. Part of the answer, as it is commonly told, came through Saudi Arabia, holder of some of the largest oil reserves in the world, whose kingdom was surrounded by enemies. The arrangements worked out in the mid-1970s, associated with Henry Kissinger and Treasury Secretary William Simon, paired American protection with Saudi oil being priced and sold in dollars and the revenues invested back into American debt. Because the whole world needed oil, the whole world needed dollars. Through OPEC, Saudi influence over oil extended across the other producing nations. That is the [[Glossary#Petrodollar|petrodollar]].

Decades of oil revenue have since been converted into influence. Saudi firms have bought farmland in the United States. Saudi Arabia is consistently among the largest foreign spenders on lobbying in Washington. Its sovereign wealth fund has taken stakes in American media and entertainment, and whoever controls the media controls the narrative. Saudi Arabia ranks among the largest foreign funders of American universities. Saudi money has built and funded mosques and religious schools all over the world. Islam is actively evangelizing in American prisons, and many prisoners convert. The share of Muslims in Europe's population continues to grow.

Islamic religious law is already operating inside the United States in some places. Islamic arbitration bodies handle family disputes among Muslims, including marriage, divorce, custody, and inheritance. The Islamic Tribunal in the Dallas area, founded in 2014, was reported in 2025 to be handling roughly three hundred cases a year. Its rulings are voluntary on paper and must be confirmed by a civil court before they can be enforced. But the pressure on members of a community to accept them is another matter. In November 2025 Texas Governor Greg Abbott called for investigations into the tribunal as a rival court system, and in April 2026 the state attorney general opened an investigation of his own; the tribunal describes itself as a voluntary mediation service.

Then there is the royal family itself. Its members travel with entourages of servants and are effectively untouchable. In 2013 a Saudi princess was arrested in Irvine, California, and charged with human trafficking for holding a Kenyan domestic worker against her will, confiscating her passport and paying her a fraction of her promised wage. Four Filipina workers were found in the same household. The charges were later dropped. The State Department placed Saudi Arabia on its lowest tier for human trafficking in 2019, citing widespread abuse of the foreign laborers the kingdom depends on. This study holds that the trade in exploited people reaches into the highest levels of Saudi society, protected by wealth and diplomatic standing from ever being held to account. That is a judgment that goes beyond what the public record can prove, and it is offered as the study's assessment, not as an established fact.

Then there is September 11. Fifteen of the nineteen hijackers were Saudi citizens. Osama bin Laden came from one of the wealthiest families in the kingdom, and his movement grew out of the Arab volunteers who joined the American-backed Afghan resistance against the Soviets. For more than a decade after the attacks, the "28 pages" of Congress's joint inquiry dealing with possible Saudi support for the hijackers stayed classified, until their release in 2016.

Like Satan, none of this operates in the open. If it did, people would see it and rebel against it. When one small country with enormous wealth exerts this kind of disproportionate influence around the world, it begins to look like the woman of Revelation 17.

### Not Rome

In this study's view, many commentaries go wrong here because of how they read Daniel. Much traditional teaching expects the Antichrist's kingdom to be a revived Rome, and the "seven hills" of Revelation 17:9 have long pointed readers to the city of Rome. But is Rome a world power today? No. Even the Roman Catholic Church, with all its wealth, authority, and influence, does not hold sway over the world the way it once did. In this study's reading, the Antichrist's kingdom rises from the lands of the caliphate, with Turkey at its heart.

### The Clue of the Sea

But the text does not say a great country. It says a great city. Is there a great city inside Saudi Arabia that fits?

Mecca and Medina come to mind first. But Revelation 18 will show that Babylon is a trading port, a city reached by sea. Neither Mecca nor Medina is on the sea. So as Revelation 18 is read, the question is: what does this city look like? What characteristics must it have? Does it have to exist right now?

## 10. Revelation 18: Fallen, Fallen Is Babylon the Great

*"After this I saw another angel coming down from heaven, having great authority, and the earth was made bright with his glory. And he called out with a mighty voice, 'Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great! She has become a dwelling place for demons, a haunt for every unclean spirit, a haunt for every unclean bird, a haunt for every unclean and detestable beast. For all nations have drunk the wine of the passion of her sexual immorality, and the kings of the earth have committed immorality with her, and the merchants of the earth have grown rich from the power of her luxurious living.'*

*Then I heard another voice from heaven saying, 'Come out of her, my people, lest you take part in her sins, lest you share in her plagues; for her sins are heaped high as heaven, and God has remembered her iniquities. Pay her back as she herself has paid back others, and repay her double for her deeds; mix a double portion for her in the cup she mixed. As she glorified herself and lived in luxury, so give her a like measure of torment and mourning, since in her heart she says, "I sit as a queen, I am no widow, and mourning I shall never see." For this reason her plagues will come in a single day, death and mourning and famine, and she will be burned up with fire; for mighty is the Lord God who has judged her.'"* (Revelation 18:1–8)

The fate of the city is settled before anything else is described. She is doomed. She will be burned with fire and completely destroyed, and she will never be rebuilt. She becomes a haunt for demons and unclean beasts, a wasteland for wild animals. Her destruction is permanent.

### Kings, Merchants, and Sailors

*"And the kings of the earth, who committed sexual immorality and lived in luxury with her, will weep and wail over her when they see the smoke of her burning. They will stand far off, in fear of her torment, and say, 'Alas! Alas! You great city, you mighty city, Babylon! For in a single hour your judgment has come.' And the merchants of the earth weep and mourn for her, since no one buys their cargo anymore, cargo of gold, silver, jewels, pearls, fine linen, purple cloth, silk, scarlet cloth, all kinds of scented wood, all kinds of articles of ivory, all kinds of articles of costly wood, bronze, iron and marble, cinnamon, spice, incense, myrrh, frankincense, wine, oil, fine flour, wheat, cattle and sheep, horses and chariots, and slaves, that is, human souls."* (Revelation 18:9–13)

Listen to that list of cargo. What comes through a modern port like Los Angeles, San Francisco, or New York? Cars, oil, electronics, the machinery of modern civilization. This list sounds old. Cattle, sheep, horses, chariots, spices, incense. In part that is because it is the luxury trade of John's own day: scholars have shown that nearly every item on it was a prized import flowing into first-century Rome.

In this study's reading, it is also a clue. It reaches back to the days of Solomon, when the Middle East was the center of the world's trade and everything moved through the Red Sea. Solomon's fleet was based on the Red Sea, and that trade was how he made his wealth (1 Kings 9:26–28; 10:22). On this reading, the great city of the Antichrist's kingdom is a throwback to that world. It also fits what the Antichrist offers. His kingdom is an economic powerhouse, and he entices people to worship him so that they can share in it.

*"'The fruit for which your soul longed has gone from you, and all your delicacies and your splendors are lost to you, never to be found again!' The merchants of these wares, who gained wealth from her, will stand far off, in fear of her torment, weeping and mourning aloud, 'Alas, alas, for the great city that was clothed in fine linen, in purple and scarlet, adorned with gold, with jewels, and with pearls! For in a single hour all this wealth has been laid waste.' And all shipmasters and seafaring men, sailors and all whose trade is on the sea, stood far off and cried out as they saw the smoke of her burning, 'What city was like the great city?' And they threw dust on their heads as they wept and mourned, crying out, 'Alas, alas, for the great city where all who had ships at sea grew rich by her wealth! For in a single hour she has been laid waste. Rejoice over her, O heaven, and you saints and apostles and prophets, for God has given judgment for you against her!'"* (Revelation 18:14–20)

Three groups mourn her: the kings who lived in luxury with her, the merchants who grew rich from her, and the shipmasters and sailors whose trade is on the sea. That last group confirms it. Babylon is a seaport.

### The Millstone

*"Then a mighty angel took up a stone like a great millstone and threw it into the sea, saying, 'So will Babylon the great city be thrown down with violence, and will be found no more; and the sound of harpists and musicians, of flute players and trumpeters, will be heard in you no more, and a craftsman of any craft will be found in you no more, and the sound of the mill will be heard in you no more, and the light of a lamp will shine in you no more, and the voice of bridegroom and bride will be heard in you no more, for your merchants were the great ones of the earth, and all nations were deceived by your sorcery. And in her was found the blood of prophets and of saints, and of all who have been slain on earth.'"* (Revelation 18:21–24)

### The Blood of the Saints

The last line adds one more characteristic. In her is found the blood of prophets and saints. The woman of chapter 17 was drunk on the blood of the martyrs of Jesus.

What happens to Christians and Jews in much of the Middle East? Converts are persecuted. Terrorist groups such as the Islamic State have burned churches and killed the people inside, and they did not kill quietly. They killed publicly and in the most barbaric ways possible, including public beheadings. Saudi Arabia is one of the very few nations that still carries out executions by beheading, and under its law the punishment for theft can be the cutting off of a hand.

## 11. Candidates on the Red Sea

The Gulf states have grown commercially at an extraordinary pace in the last ten years. The UAE, Qatar, and Saudi Arabia are awash in money. They are diversifying away from oil and into tourism and sports leagues, and above all into influence, brokering their oil money for influence around the world.

What follows is food for thought, not a fixed identification.

Saudi Arabia has a great port city on the Red Sea: Jeddah, the gateway to Mecca and the kingdom's center of trade. The Jeddah Tower now rising there is designed to become the tallest building in the world.

There is another possibility. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman's signature project is [[Glossary#NEOM|NEOM]], a planned region of new cities in the northwest of the kingdom along the Red Sea. Parts of it, such as the ski resort and The Line, a linear city in the desert, have been put on hold or scaled back. As of 2026, the part still reported to be receiving investment is the port city, Oxagon, an eight-sided industrial and logistics city built partly floating on the Red Sea, planned around hydrogen production, massive AI data centers, and trade. It is designed to be a center of trade.

### Two Bodies of Water

If trade is centered in the Middle East, two bodies of water have to be controlled. At the time of this session in 2026, Iran was showing what a chokepoint is worth, effectively closing and then policing the Strait of Hormuz in the war that began when the United States and Israel struck Iran on February 28, 2026. If all the trade runs through the Middle East, the most important body of water in the region becomes the Red Sea, and the second most important is the Mediterranean.

Which Middle Eastern country has one of the strongest navies in the region? Turkey, which sits over the Mediterranean and has NATO's second-largest army. Put Saudi Arabia and Turkey together and you have religious legitimacy, a trade center, and military power under one alignment. On August 7, 2026, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and Pakistan signed the [[Glossary#Mecca Joint Defense Agreement|Mecca Joint Defense Agreement]], treating an attack on one as an attack on all three. It built on a Saudi-Pakistani mutual defense pact signed in September 2025, and Pakistan brings the only nuclear arsenal in the Muslim world. The three are already religiously aligned and economically aligned, and in this study's reading that agreement may prove to be the beginning of what Revelation describes.

The alliance also leaves Egypt out. Egypt and Turkey have drawn closer in recent years, and Egypt may have reason to feel slighted. According to Daniel, the first nation to attack the Antichrist is the [[Glossary#King of the North / King of the South|king of the South]], Egypt. The Antichrist crushes it and carries off the treasures of Egypt (Daniel 11:40–43). Laid side by side, the political pieces begin to fit the prophecy.

### Sunni and Shia

Iran fits differently from Turkey, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt, and the difference is religious. Islam is split into two main branches, [[Glossary#Sunni and Shia|Sunni and Shia]]. Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and most of the Islamic world are Sunni. The Shia are a smaller share, but Iraq is majority Shia, even though Saddam Hussein was a Sunni, and Iran is overwhelmingly Shia. Iran has been the great counterweight to Sunni power in the region, and the U.S. State Department has long designated it the world's leading state sponsor of terrorism.

What was happening to Iran at the time of this session? Its power was being diminished by war. Politically and religiously, that removes the counterweight and allows the Sunni powers to rise.

### Why Saudi Arabia Looks to Turkey

Why would Mohammed bin Salman, one of the most powerful royals in the Middle East, align himself with Turkey? Saudi Arabia has excellent equipment, and the United States has trained its forces for years, but in this study's assessment its military does not have what it takes. In the Middle East, military strength is everything, and Turkey has one of the most powerful militaries in the region.

There is a religious side as well. In Turkey, some Islamist voices have openly called for the caliphate to be restored, and the centenary of its abolition in 2024 brought those calls to the surface. The caliphate is the political government; the Mahdi is the one figure who will unite the Islamic world under it. Right now the Middle East is divided into separate states. As the Antichrist rises, all of those kings will give him their power, and one man will be in charge of all that land, all those peoples, and all those states, united by religion.

Something else has shifted. Saudi Arabia has long relied on the United States for its security, yet when it looked for protection in 2025 and 2026, it went to Turkey and to Pakistan. That is a sea change from the last fifty years.

## 12. A Subtle and Insidious System

The economic ground is moving as well. The BRICS nations (Brazil, Russia, India, China, and South Africa, with others joining) are seeking ways to trade outside the dollar. The United States shapes behavior through the dollar: through sanctions, through the network banks use to move money between countries, and through freezing assets, as the United States and its allies froze roughly $300 billion of Russia's central-bank reserves in 2022. Nations have responded by stockpiling gold, the one form of money that cannot be frozen by another sovereign country, and by exploring other currencies for oil. Saudi Arabia has explored selling oil to China outside the dollar.

Will Satan set up something overt and singular? In this study's reading, no. His tentacles are everywhere. This will be very subtle and insidious. It will not broadcast itself as it comes about, and it will happen in the shadows.

> **Key Principle:** Satan does not build his kingdom in the open. What Revelation describes will arrive quietly, through influence, money, and alliances, long before it is visible to anyone not watching for it.

## 13. Watching the World With the Text in Hand

Mainstream outlets give little coverage to the progress of the temple preparations in Jerusalem or the work of the Temple Institute. So the discipline is to know what to look for and search for it directly: the latest on the Temple Institute and a rebuilt temple, the alliances forming among the nations of the region, the voices of missionaries and sources embedded in the Middle East. No single source tells the whole story. The work is connecting them.

Part of what makes this difficult is that Americans, living in a superpower, often know little about the world around them. Much of this is simply history, and it is a history most Americans have never been taught. A day is coming when that will no longer be possible.

Through all of it, a distinction has to be kept. Much of this session has been history and current events, and none of it is Scripture. There are many things the Bible says, and many it does not. Where it speaks, it is certain, and that certainty is the standard by which everything else is measured.

> **Key Principle:** Hold what Scripture says with certainty, and hold every identification of current nations and cities loosely. The text is fixed; the reading of the news is not.
